Who We Are

SecureBank is a leading retail bank serving over two million customers across France and Europe. Founded in Lyon in 1987, we have built a reputation for trust, transparency, and financial innovation.

Our 4 000 employees work every day to provide the best banking experience — in branch, online, or through our mobile app.

Security Commitment

At SecureBank we take the security of your data and finances extremely seriously. Our systems are protected by industry-standard encryption and are audited annually by independent third-party firms.

We comply fully with GDPR, PSD2, and all relevant European banking regulations.
